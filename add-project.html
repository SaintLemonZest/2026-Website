Add your project to the database

Use the project form link sent to you by email to submit your project details. You do not need to edit the website or database yourself.

How to submit the form

  1. Open the project form using the link in the email. Check that it is the form from your teacher or project organiser.
  2. Enter your name and email address so your submission can be identified if anything needs checking.
  3. Add your project title and select the category that best matches it: CAD Modelling, Website Development, or Game Development.
  4. Choose the project status shown in the form. Select the option that accurately describes where your project is now.
  5. Describe what your project is for, who it is intended for, and what it does. Include enough detail for someone unfamiliar with it to understand.
  6. List the materials, software, tools, or technologies you are using. If the form asks when the project was created, enter the date as requested.
  7. If the form requests images, add up to the stated limit (the project form may allow up to five). Choose clear images of your design or outcome. If you need to provide links, make sure the intended viewers can open them.
  8. Review your answers, then use the form's submit button. Look for an on-screen confirmation before closing it.

Before you submit

  • Check names, spelling, dates, and links for accuracy.
  • Only include information and images that are appropriate to share on the project website. Do not include passwords or other private information.
  • If you cannot access the form, need to correct a submission, or are unsure what to enter, reply to the email or ask your teacher for help rather than submitting someone else's details.

Your form response is used to prepare a project entry for the DT Hub. If the entry does not appear or needs changing, contact the teacher or organiser who sent the form.
